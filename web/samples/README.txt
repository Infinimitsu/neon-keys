Neon Keys sample songs - one of each kind the song editor can read. All are public domain.
In Neon Keys: Lessons > My Songs > + Add a song, then either "Load an example..." or
"Open a MIDI or text file..." and pick one of these.

  mary-guitar-tab.txt         guitar tab (6 lines, one per string)
  twinkle-piano-tab.txt       piano tab (one line per octave: 4|c-d-e-|)
  twinkle-chord-sheet.txt     chord sheet: [C]words [F]more words (the words come along automatically)
  frere-jacques-letters.txt   letter notes with the words in between (E D C D...)
  jingle-bells.mid            MIDI file with melody, bass and drums ("Melody only" keeps the tune)
  *-lyrics.txt                the words for each, split into syllables - paste into the Lyrics box

For a song with no tab or MIDI file online: in the song editor press "Record on the keyboard",
play the tune on the keyboard while listening to it, press Stop, then type in the words.
